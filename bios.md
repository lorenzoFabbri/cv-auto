## Long bio (GitHub profile and website)

I'm **Lorenzo Fabbri**, a cancer epidemiologist at the Spanish National Cancer Research Centre (CNIO) in Madrid, where I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Genetic and Molecular Epidemiology Group and hold a Juan de la Cierva fellowship (2026–2029). My research asks what medicines do to the risk of cancer. I estimate the effect of starting one drug rather than another by emulating target trials on routine health records, mainly in pancreatic and bladder cancer, and report each result across social groups where the data allow.

My training is in environmental epidemiology and causal inference. For my PhD in Biomedicine at Universitat Pompeu Fabra (2021–2025), in the Childhood and Environment group at ISGlobal under Prof. Martine Vrijheid and Dr. Léa Maitre, I studied early-life exposure to environmental chemicals and child neurodevelopment in the HELIX and ATHLETE cohorts, using the parametric g-formula, evidence triangulation and multi-omic data. Between the PhD and CNIO I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Rakislova Lab at ISGlobal, working on the cancer genomics of vulvar and penile squamous cell carcinoma.

I am also studying for further degrees in public health (UNED), social inequalities (UNED), theoretical statistics (the Open University) and economics (UOC). I write open-source software for causal inference and reproducible research: I lead [etverse](https://etverse.github.io/), an R ecosystem for transparent causal inference, maintain [forrest](https://cran.r-project.org/package=forrest) and [orcidtr](https://cran.r-project.org/package=orcidtr) on CRAN, and wrote [bibaudit](https://pypi.org/project/bibaudit/), a Python package that checks a bibliography field by field against Crossref, DataCite and PubMed.

### Summary of CV

I am a cancer epidemiologist. Since June 2026 I have been a postdoctoral researcher in the Genetic and Molecular Epidemiology Group of Dr. Núria Malats at the Spanish National Cancer Research Centre (CNIO, Madrid), and I was awarded a Juan de la Cierva postdoctoral fellowship (Agencia Estatal de Investigación, ref. JDC2025-058904-I) for December 2026 to November 2029, held through CIBER at CNIO. My line is causal pharmacoepidemiology in cancer: I estimate what medicines do to the risk of cancer by emulating target trials on primary-care records and on the group's case-control studies, mainly in pancreatic and bladder cancer, and report each result across social groups where the data allow. Before CNIO I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Rakislova Lab at ISGlobal (October 2025 to March 2026), working on the cancer genomics of vulvar and penile squamous cell carcinoma from whole-exome sequencing.

I completed my PhD in Biomedicine at Universitat Pompeu Fabra (defended 19/12/2025, Sobresaliente cum laude) in the Childhood and Environment group at ISGlobal, supervised by Prof. Martine Vrijheid and Dr. Léa Maitre. The thesis studied prenatal and childhood exposure to mixtures of environmental chemicals and neurodevelopment in the HELIX and ATHLETE cohorts, using the parametric g-formula, evidence triangulation and multi-omic integration. My record has eight journal articles, three as first author, in Environment International, Environmental Research, Nature Communications, JAMA Network Open, International Journal of Obesity, BMJ Open and PLOS Computational Biology, and a manuscript on clonal evolution in penile squamous cell carcinoma is in preparation. I received the ISEE Students and New Researchers Network Best Abstract Award (Student Researchers) in 2022 and gave an invited talk on transparent causal inference to the Colicino group at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai in 2025.

Over the fellowship I aim to lead studies of medicines and cancer risk with protocols registered before analysis, to apply for a project grant as principal investigator, and to co-supervise a doctoral student. My background is in biotechnology (University of Parma) and quantitative and computational biology (M.Sc., University of Trento, 110/110 cum laude), with research stays at Fondazione Bruno Kessler, the Heidelberg Institute for Theoretical Studies and RWTH Aachen University. I completed the UNED University Expert Diploma in Advanced Methods of Applied Statistics (2026) and am enrolled in the UNED Lifelong Learning Master's in Public Health, the UNED Master's in the Analysis of Social Inequalities, the UOC Master's in Economic Analysis and the Open University Graduate Certificate in Theoretical Statistics and Probability.

I write open-source software for causal inference and reproducible research: I lead etverse, a modular R ecosystem for transparent causal inference, maintain the CRAN packages forrest and orcidtr, and wrote bibaudit, a Python package that verifies bibliographies against Crossref, DataCite and PubMed. I have reviewed for Scientific Reports, belong to the Cancer Working Group of the Sociedad Española de Epidemiología, took part in the ISEE Students and New Researchers Network (2022–2023) and the COMETS Early Career Scientist Working Group (2022), and am a member of the European Association for Cancer Research, ALIPANC and the Sociedad Española de Epidemiología.

Postdoctoral research in the Genetic and Molecular Epidemiology Group (principal investigator: Dr. Núria Malats).
